# ProvableLearning — Parent Guide

> Last Updated: 2026-04-12
> Audience: Parents using the ProvableLearning tutoring platform

---

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Go to the ProvableLearning website and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your email and create a password (minimum 6 characters), or sign up with **Google**
3. Complete onboarding: enter your full name, phone number, and select **Parent** as your role
4. You'll be taken to your Parent Dashboard

### Logging In

- Use your email + password, or click **Sign in with Google**
- If you signed up with Google, you must continue using Google to log in (and vice versa)
- Forgot your password? Click **Forgot Password** on the login page to receive a reset link via email

---

## Managing Your Students

### Adding a Student

1. From your dashboard, click **Add Student**
2. Enter the student's full name, grade level (6–12), email address, and an initial password
3. The student can now log in independently with their own email and password

### Student Password Reset

You can reset your student's password directly from the dashboard — no need for the old password.

### Removing a Student

You can remove a student from your account via the dashboard. This does not cancel their enrollments.

## Class Types

ProvableLearning offers three class formats:

| | Large Group | Small Group | 1-on-1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $20/month | $300/month | $800/month |
| **Sessions** | 2x per week | 2 slots per week (you pick) | 2x per week |
| **Duration** | 1.5 hours | 1.5 hours | 1.5 hours |
| **Cancellation** | Not available | Yes (24h notice) | Yes (anytime) |
| **Makeups** | Not available | Yes — book any open SG slot | Yes — book any open 1:1 slot |
| **Recordings** | Available to watch | N/A | N/A |

### Subjects

When enrolling, you choose the subject for your student:
- **DSAT Reading & Writing**
- **DSAT Math**
- **DSAT Reading, Writing & Math**
- **School Math** (specify the course, e.g., Algebra 2)

The subject is tied to the enrollment, not the class time slot. Small Group and 1-on-1 slots are subject-agnostic — your student will receive instruction in their enrolled subject during any slot they attend.

## Enrolling in a Class

### Browsing Classes

1. Go to **Enroll** from the navigation
2. Browse available classes organized by subject
3. Filter by meeting day and time
4. Each listing shows current enrollment count and capacity

### Enrollment Steps

1. Select a class
2. For **Small Group / 1-on-1**: choose 2 preferred weekly time slots
3. For **Large Group**: enroll directly (single class)
4. Read and accept the enrollment agreement
5. Choose payment:
   - **Pay Now** — redirects to Stripe checkout (card or bank transfer)
   - **Pay Later** — enrollment is active immediately, payment due within 7 days of your student's start date

### If a Class Is Full

You can join the **waitlist**. When a spot opens:
- **Large Group**: you are auto-enrolled
- **Small Group / 1-on-1**: you receive an offer notification with a 3-day window to accept. If you don't respond, the spot goes to the next person in line.

## Payments

### Payment Options

- **Credit/Debit Card**: processed through Stripe at checkout
- **ACH Bank Transfer**: available at checkout (takes a few days to clear)

### Pay Later

If you chose "Pay Later" at enrollment, your student is enrolled immediately but payment is due within **7 days** of their start date. You can pay anytime from the **Payments** page on your dashboard by clicking **Pay Now** next to the unpaid enrollment.

### What Happens If You Don't Pay

If payment is not received by the deadline, the enrollment is **automatically cancelled**. The freed spot goes to the next student on the waitlist.

### Payment History

View all enrollment payment statuses on the **Payments** page — including class name, dates, amount, and status (paid/unpaid/refunded).

## Session Cancellation

### Rules by Class Type

| Class Type | Can Cancel? | Notice Required | What You Get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large Group | No | — | Watch recordings instead |
| Small Group | Yes | 24 hours before session | Makeup session or credit |
| 1-on-1 | Yes | No minimum notice | Makeup session or credit |

### How to Cancel a Session

1. Go to **Cancel Session** from your dashboard
2. Select the student and enrollment
3. Choose the specific session to cancel
4. Confirm the cancellation

After cancelling, you can immediately book a **makeup session** or let the system generate a **credit** after 7 days.

---

## Makeup Sessions

### Booking a Makeup

After cancelling a session, you can:

1. **Book immediately**: browse available sessions in other classes of the same group size. You can book any open slot within your enrollment period.
2. **Join the makeup waitlist**: if no slots are available, you'll be notified when one opens up.

Makeup sessions are **subject-agnostic** — your student attends a different time slot but receives instruction in their enrolled subject.

### If You Don't Book a Makeup

If 7 days pass after a cancelled session without booking a makeup, the cancellation converts to a **credit** that you can redeem later.

---

## Credits

Credits are generated when:
- A cancelled session goes 7 days without a makeup booking
- A makeup booking is missed (no-show) — the credit is restored

### Redeeming Credits

1. Go to **Redeem Credit** from your dashboard
2. View your credit balance by group size (Large, Small, 1-on-1)
3. Browse available future sessions
4. Select a session to book with your credit

Credits match by group size type — a Small Group credit can only be used for a Small Group makeup slot.

## Dropping a Class

Dropping a class is **permanent**. The consequences depend on timing:

### Phase 1 — More than 14 days before start date
- Self-serve drop available
- Only for unpaid enrollments

### Phase 2 — Within 14 days before start to 7 days after start
- A note is recorded with reason
- Future re-enrollment in the same class is blocked
- Credit may be issued

### Phase 3 — More than 7 days after start
- Self-serve drop is not available
- You must schedule a **refund consultation** with the team

### Paid Enrollments

If you've already paid, you cannot self-drop. You must schedule a refund consultation to discuss options.

### How to Drop

1. Go to **Drop Class** from your dashboard
2. Select the student and enrollment
3. Provide a reason
4. Confirm the drop

---

## Refund Requests

For paid enrollments, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis:
1. Go to **Refund** from your dashboard
2. You'll be directed to schedule a consultation with the team to discuss your situation

## Google Classroom & Meet

### Accessing Classes

After enrollment, you'll receive:
- A **Google Meet link** for joining live sessions
- A **Google Classroom** link/code for class materials

For **Small Group and 1-on-1**: a classroom is created specifically for your student.
For **Large Group**: your student joins the shared class classroom.

### External Email Users

If your student uses a personal Gmail (not a school/workspace account), they may need to self-join the classroom using the **enrollment code** provided on the dashboard.

## Notifications & Messaging

### Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:
- Enrollment confirmations
- Payment reminders and deadlines
- Waitlist offers (when a spot opens)
- Makeup booking confirmations
- Session reminders

Notifications appear on your dashboard and are sent via email. SMS notifications are available for consultation booking reminders if you opt in.

### Messaging

You can send messages directly to the tutor/admin from your dashboard. View your message history and any responses in the same place.
