# ProvableLearning — Student Guide

> Last Updated: 2026-04-12
> Audience: Students using the ProvableLearning tutoring platform

---

## Getting Started

### How You Get an Account

There are two ways:

1. **Parent creates your account**: Your parent adds you from their dashboard with your name, email, grade, and a password. You can then log in on your own.
2. **You sign up independently** (age 18+): Go to the website, click **Sign Up**, and create your own account. Select **Student** as your role and enter your grade level.

You can log in with your email + password, or with **Google**.

### First Login

When you log in for the first time, you'll complete a short onboarding:
- Confirm your name and phone number
- Select your grade level (6–12)

After that, you'll land on your **Student Dashboard**.

---

## Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:

- **Your enrolled classes** — class name, subject, meeting days/times, Google Meet + Classroom links
- **Performance** — attendance and homework completion for each session
- **Credit balance** — available makeup credits by group size
- **Notifications** — enrollment confirmations, reminders, waitlist offers
- **Messages** — conversations with your tutor/admin

---

## What You Can vs. Can't Do

| Action | Independent Student | Student with Parent |
|---|---|---|
| Enroll in a class | Yes | Yes (parent can also enroll you) |
| Cancel a session | Yes | Yes (parent can also do this) |
| Book a makeup | Yes | Yes |
| Redeem a credit | Yes | Yes |
| Drop a class | Yes | Yes (parent can also do this) |
| Pay for enrollment | Yes | Parent typically handles this |
| Add another student | No | No (parent only) |

If you have a parent account linked, both you and your parent can manage your enrollments.

## Class Types

| | Large Group | Small Group | 1-on-1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $20/month | $300/month | $800/month |
| **Sessions** | 2x per week | 2 slots per week (you pick) | 2x per week |
| **Duration** | 1.5 hours each | 1.5 hours each | 1.5 hours each |
| **Can cancel?** | No | Yes (24h notice) | Yes (anytime) |
| **Makeups?** | No | Yes | Yes |

### Subjects

Your subject is chosen at enrollment:
- **DSAT Reading & Writing**
- **DSAT Math**
- **DSAT Reading, Writing & Math**
- **School Math** (specify course, e.g., Algebra 2)

Small Group and 1-on-1 time slots are subject-agnostic — you'll get instruction in your enrolled subject no matter which slot you attend.

## Enrolling in a Class

1. Go to **Enroll** from the navigation
2. Browse available classes and filter by day/time
3. Select a class
4. For **Small Group / 1-on-1**: choose 2 preferred weekly time slots
5. For **Large Group**: enroll directly
6. Accept the enrollment agreement
7. Choose **Pay Now** (Stripe checkout) or **Pay Later** (7-day deadline)

### If the Class Is Full

Join the **waitlist**:
- **Large Group**: you're auto-enrolled when a spot opens
- **Small Group / 1-on-1**: you get a notification with a 3-day window to accept the spot

---

## Payments

### Pay Now
Pay immediately at enrollment via credit/debit card or bank transfer (ACH).

### Pay Later
Your enrollment is active right away, but payment must be made within **7 days** of your start date. Go to **Payments** on your dashboard and click **Pay Now** next to the unpaid enrollment.

### If You Don't Pay
Your enrollment is **automatically cancelled** after the deadline. The spot goes to the next student on the waitlist.

## Cancelling a Session

### When You Can Cancel

| Class Type | Can Cancel? | Notice Required |
|---|---|---|
| Large Group | No | — |
| Small Group | Yes | 24 hours before the session |
| 1-on-1 | Yes | No minimum |

### How to Cancel

1. Go to **Cancel Session** from your dashboard
2. Select the enrollment and session
3. Confirm

After cancelling, you can book a makeup or let it convert to a credit after 7 days.

---

## Makeup Sessions

After cancelling a session:

1. **Book a makeup**: Browse open slots in other classes of the same group size. Pick any available session within your enrollment period.
2. **Join the makeup waitlist**: If nothing is available, you'll be notified when a slot opens.

Makeups are subject-agnostic — you attend a different time slot but get help with your enrolled subject.

### If You Don't Book

After 7 days without booking a makeup, your cancellation automatically converts to a **credit**.

---

## Credits

Credits are for booking makeup sessions later. They're generated when:
- A cancelled session goes 7 days without a makeup booking
- A makeup no-show occurs (the credit is restored)

### How to Use Credits

1. Go to **Redeem Credit** from your dashboard
2. See your balance by group size (Large, Small, 1-on-1)
3. Browse available future sessions
4. Select one to book

Credits are matched by group size — a Small Group credit works only for Small Group slots.

---

## Dropping a Class

Dropping is **permanent**. What happens depends on timing:

| Phase | When | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | More than 14 days before start | Self-serve drop (unpaid only) |
| Phase 2 | 14 days before start → 7 days after start | Note + cancellation, blocks re-enrollment |
| Phase 3 | More than 7 days after start | Must schedule a refund consultation |

If you've already paid, you cannot self-drop — a refund consultation is required.

### How to Drop

1. Go to **Drop Class** from your dashboard
2. Select the enrollment
3. Provide a reason
4. Confirm

---

## Google Classroom & Meet

After enrolling, your dashboard shows:
- **Google Meet link** — click to join live sessions
- **Google Classroom link/code** — access class materials

For Small Group and 1-on-1, a classroom is created just for you. For Large Group, you join the shared classroom.

If you use a personal Gmail, you may need to self-join using the **enrollment code** shown on your dashboard.
